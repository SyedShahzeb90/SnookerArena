# Business Rules

## Sessions

- Tables can be Available, Running, Payment Pending, Reserved or Maintenance.
- A session may start immediately or from a custom past time.
- Future start times are not allowed.
- Sessions can be edited before payment.
- Closed sessions can be reopened before payment.
- Paid sessions become locked.

---

## Billing

- Game charges and cafe charges belong to the same bill.
- Bills remain open until payment is received.
- Additional cafe items can be added before payment.
- Payment can happen later.

---

## Cafe

- Customers may order from the cafe without playing.
- Cafe orders can later be assigned to a table.
- Multiple cafe items belong to the same order.

---

## Simplicity Rules

- Customer name is optional.
- Never require unnecessary information.
- Cashier should perform common tasks in under 10 seconds.
- Every screen should remain clean and easy to understand.
